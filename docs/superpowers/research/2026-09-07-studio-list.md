# Polish hourly photo / content studios — desk research (2026-09-07)

## Method

- Candidates came from 12 web searches ("studio fotograficzne do wynajęcia <city> cennik", "wynajem studia fotograficznego <city> godzina", "cyklorama wynajem", "bookero/calendesk studio fotograficzne") across Warszawa, Kraków, Wrocław, Poznań, Trójmiasto, Łódź, Katowice, Rzeszów. Portals (fotoszukacz, bookfolino, venuu, rentalify, lento, pxly) were used only to find names, never as evidence.
- Every studio's OWN site was read on 2026-09-07: homepage + cennik + rezerwacja + regulamin/FAQ. Pages were pulled with `curl` (raw HTML) so booking widgets/iframes/scripts could be grepped for Bookero, Calendesk, Booksy, Reservio, SimplyBook, Nakiedy, Fitssey, Calendly, Zencal, Setmore, Google Forms, form plugins, `tel:`/`mailto:`/Instagram links; text was extracted from the same HTML. JS-only pages were rendered with headless Playwright or read through the site's own WordPress REST API (`/wp-json`) where noted; a Google-Doc regulamin was read via text export. Reading was split across four parallel readers (one per city group) and 8 claims were re-checked by hand against the raw pages.
- Cells hold only what a page says; "n/s" = not stated on any page read. Where the cennik and the regulamin disagree, both values are given. Prices are PLN per hour unless noted; "net"/"gross" only when the page says netto/brutto.
- Cap: 46 studios verified; the table keeps the first 40 by the requested city priority (Warszawa → Katowice). The other 6 (Rzeszów ×4, Bielsko-Biała, Bytom) are in the appendix and are NOT in the counts.
- Counting rules used below: "≥3 rooms" = three or more separately named, separately bookable spaces; "tiered cancellation" = at least two time thresholds with graded charges (a single free-before-X-else-100% rule is listed as "single"); "combos" = whole-studio/multi-room hire or equipment/lamps/machines/cameras sold as priced extras (make-up rooms, assistants, backdrops/props are recorded but not counted); "after-session" = overtime rounding, cleaning/confetti fee, or damage/repaint fee mentioned (paper-backdrop consumption alone does not count).
